Why Gutsy

Most apps flag red.
Gutsy explains why.

Generic traffic-light labels don't know your gut. Gutsy scores every product against your personal profile — and shows its work.

A score built around your gut

During onboarding, Gutsy learns your bloating patterns, trigger foods, digestion, and energy. Every scan is measured against that profile — so the same yogurt can score differently for you than for anyone else.

Bloating · Skin · Digestion · Energy Four of the seven dimensions scored on every single product.

Scan a barcode or the label itself

Point your camera at a barcode for instant lookup, or photograph the ingredient panel and Gutsy reads it — additives, sugar aliases, seed oils, all of it.

Better alternatives, ranked

Every low-scoring scan comes with cleaner swaps in the same category — so you always leave with something you can actually buy instead.
